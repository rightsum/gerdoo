The robot stands on the two drive wheels and two 1" swivel casters. Each caster wheel trails 10 mm behind its pivot and swings round as you drive, so the support diamond changes shape: after driving forward the front contact sits 80 mm ahead of the axle, after reversing 100 mm. Braking pushes the centre of gravity forward by its height × deceleration / g; the limit is where it reaches the front contact.

What this model is
  • Body: every visible part of the Fusion file gerdoo-assembly-preview, exported at low resolution, including the new covers and the 3rd floor with the speaker grille.
  • Arms: ElRobot follower URDF from norma-core, mounted with J1 at ±145.7 mm, on gerdoo's own round arm bases.
  • Contacts: mesh-exact checks of every arm part against the body, the other arm and the floor. A part of one arm is not checked against the same arm, so J1 stops at ±88.9°, where its yoke meets the housing.
  • Mass model: printed parts from their Fusion volumes, arm links from the ElRobot URDF, the rest from datasheets or estimates (sim/web/tools/physics.py).
  • Dock: charging-dock-v1; the robot starts on it. Drive forward to leave, and back into it within 4 mm and 3° to charge.
  • Casters: 1" soft-rubber swivel casters, 25 mm wheel, 10 mm trail (typical; measure yours). The listing's 37.8 mm height is 1.3 mm more than the gap under the caster blocks, so the sim fits them to 36.5 mm.
  • Approximate: the neck gimbal's two axes, the Brio, and the scan height. Wheels roll without slipping and there is no physics.
